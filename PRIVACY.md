# TGVault Privacy Policy

TGVault is a browser extension that stores your files in your own Telegram Saved Messages, encrypted in your browser.

**What the developer collects:** nothing. TGVault does not send any data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer, and it has no analytics or tracking.

**What the extension handles, and where it goes:**
- Your phone number, login code and (if you use one) Telegram two-step password are entered by you and sent directly to Telegram's servers to log you in.
- Your Telegram login session is stored on your own device (in the browser's extension storage) so you stay signed in. It is deleted when you log out.
- Your files and photos are encrypted in your browser with a passphrase only you know, then uploaded directly to your own Telegram Saved Messages. File names and folder names are encrypted too. The developer cannot read your files and cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.
- A short key-check value is stored on your device to detect a mistyped passphrase.
- Password-locked share files are created entirely on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere by TGVault.

**Third parties:** the only third party involved is Telegram, which receives your data because you use your own Telegram account. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to that data. TGVault does not sell data and does not share it with anyone else.

**Your control:** logging out removes your session and encryption key from the browser. You can delete files at any time from within TGVault, which removes them from Saved Messages.
